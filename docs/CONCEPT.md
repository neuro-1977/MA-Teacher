# Product concept

MA-Teacher is a private-by-default, teacher-led learning workspace. It connects reviewed curriculum evidence, lesson decisions, supervised practice, submitted work, human feedback, and progress records without pretending that generated or extracted material is automatically correct.

The public preview targets science, English, mathematics, history, languages, and computing or information technology across age groups. Coverage varies and must be checked against the learner, jurisdiction, stage, and current authoritative source.

The teacher remains responsible for curriculum decisions, safeguarding, accessibility, and marking. The student receives lessons, submits work, and reads human feedback. Automatic tutoring and grading are not part of version 0.1.0.
