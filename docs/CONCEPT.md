# MA-Teacher Concept

## Status

**Roadmap only.** This project has no settled product name and no feature build
has begun.

## Purpose

An English National Curriculum student/teacher mobile companion. It should make
curriculum progression understandable to learners and useful to teachers without
turning the first release into a generic school administration system.

## Product roles

- **Student:** a clear, age-appropriate view of learning goals, current topics,
  progress, and safe next actions.
- **Teacher:** a practical view of curriculum intent, class or learner progress,
  and planning context.

## Design constraints

- Keep student and teacher surfaces distinct.
- Local-first is the default for future data handling.
- Curriculum source material, safeguarding, permissions, assessment rules, and
  any accounts require explicit design and approval before implementation.
- Do not claim official curriculum endorsement or coverage until the relevant
  source material has been verified and mapped.

## First future decisions

1. Choose the product name.
2. Define the supported key stage and subject boundary.
3. Decide whether the first useful workflow is student planning, teacher
   planning, or a tightly scoped shared view.
4. Establish the canonical curriculum-source and safeguarding model.
